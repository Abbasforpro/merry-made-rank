Privacy policy

Merry Made Rank is a free keyword research tool for Etsy sellers, run by Abbas Pro (muhammadabbasenv@gmail.com).

What it collects: nothing about visitors. It has no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Settings you change are saved only in your own browser.

Data it reads: public Etsy listing data from the Etsy Open API, and keyword search volume from the Google Ads API using the owner's own Google Ads account. Keywords you type are sent to these services to get results. No Google user data from visitors is accessed or stored.
